Upload a two-column table (code, term). Each term is mapped to its best SNOMED CT concept through the semantic channel (no rerank, no LLM) — the right regime for terminology-to-terminology maps, where source terms are already curated clinical phrases. Two columns are appended (snomed_code, snomed_term) and you download the result.
